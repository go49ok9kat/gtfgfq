Giving a thoughtful compliment can brighten someone’s day—and build stronger connections. Whether you’re complimenting a friend, partner, coworker, or even a stranger, the key is to be genuine, specific, and kind. A well-placed compliment shows that you’re paying attention and that you care.

Be sincere. Say what you actually admire about the person—it’ll feel more meaningful.

Get specific. Instead of “You’re great,” try “I love how you always find the silver lining.”

Use body language. A warm smile or eye contact helps your words land more authentically.
