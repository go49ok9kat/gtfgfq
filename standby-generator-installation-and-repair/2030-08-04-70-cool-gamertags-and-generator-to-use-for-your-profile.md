Whether you’re a console user or PC devotee, every gamer needs an awesome gamertag for their online profile. A great gamertag is memorable, unique-sounding, and personalized to you and your interests (or your sense of humor). So, we’ve created the ultimate generator to create cool gamertags for you—and if that’s not enough, we’ve also included plenty of premade suggestions and gamertag ideas that you can use. Let’s get started! Cool Gamertag Examples

Cool gamertags range in style and can be edgy, cyber-inspired, or funny. Here are a few types: GlyphMaster lordofchaos CyberSpecter KillerKumquat kingpinz QuantumQuake LootnScoot Steps Section 1 of 8: Cool Online & MMO Gamertag Ideas

Are you big on MMOs (massively multiplayer online games) or mega-popular other online games, like Fortnite or Roblox? If that’s your jam, your gamertag could include a reference to your favorite online game, or commonly-used jargon by you and your fellow gamers. Mix and match different words you like to create the perfect gamertag for your playstyle! “Heroic” is more of a vibe than anything else, but we thought it’d be fitting, since so many games put you in the role of a protagonist (or, alternatively, a really good fighter) and let you be your most heroic virtual self. So, try putting together a gamertag that also sounds heroic—or just plain hardcore. Everyone loves a clever play on words or a gamer-related pun, right? A funny gamertag can reflect your unique sense of humor, as well as your gaming interests (as in the types of games you enjoy playing). When in doubt, look to pop culture for gamertag inspiration! “Pop culture,” of course, applies to everything from the movies, shows, and other media that are all the rage to funny memes and internet jargon. Whatever aspect of pop culture appeals most to you, try working it into your gamertag. For example: OutofController unfriendme iatepacman RebelScum oneofcharliesangels bigfootisreal FastandtheCurious captainobvious PawneeGoddess MemesRUs Section 5 of 8: Cool Myth-Inspired Gamertag Ideas

Think outside the box when it comes to your gamertag—especially if you want something unique and memorable. In fact, try stepping away from modern lingo altogether; you might just find the inspiration you need in the iconic creatures from mythology around the world. As we’ve mentioned above, the type of game you most enjoy playing can be a huge inspiration for your gamertag. So, if RPGs (roleplaying games) are nearest and dearest to your heart, you can show it with a gamertag referencing a specific RPG or the genre as a whole. Try playing around with different RPG buzzwords, characters, and game titles to come up with your gamertag. For example: assassins_bleed NightBlade dragonryyder CyberSpecter templartrash titan_slayer stickyswashbucker GalacticOutlaw theSoleSurvivor wildnwitchery Section 7 of 8: Cool FPS-Inspired Gamertag Ideas

If you’re a hardcore FPS (first-person shooter) gamer, playing the likes of Valorant, Apex Legends, Call of Duty, Overwatch, and beyond, you might want a gamertag to match. After all, people should know what a wicked shot you are! Put together a gamertag using your favorite FPS as inspiration. For instance: headshooter kingpinz noobslayyyer snipe_the_hype CovertDestroyer SeeknDestroy ultimatecheezing ms-grenade killerQueen Combo_king CoDfather Advertisement Section 8 of 8: What makes a cool gamertag?

A cool gamertag should incorporate aspects of your online identity. There’s really no wrong or right way to make a cool gamertag for yourself, as long as the end result is something that really speaks to you in one way or another—whether you take inspiration from your hobbies, favorite games, or your own sense of humor. The goal is to create something unique and memorable that tells people a little bit about who you are. It can include the following:[2]XResearch source

Humor - Relatable jokes and puns can make you seem more “approachable,” even when people can’t see your face.

Pop culture references - Referencing your favorite celebrity, game, or other pop culture reference can make your username more engaging, as well as highlight your interests.
