This wikiHow guide teaches you how to back up your Android contacts to a Google account or a VCF file. Things You Should Know

You can back up Android contacts to your Google account in Settings > Google > Settings for Google Apps > Google Contacts Sync.

You can also back up contacts to a VCF file by exporting them from the Contacts app.

Once you create a VCF file for exported contacts, you can save the file to an SD card, a folder on your phone, or your Google Drive. Steps Method 1 Method 1 of 2: Backing Up to a Google Account Download Article 1 Open your Android's Settings
