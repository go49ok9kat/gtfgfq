Did you know you can get internet access on your computer using a hotspot? Whether you want to use your iPhone, Android, or public hotspot, it's easy to connect. This wikiHow article teaches you how to connect your computer to a mobile hotspot. Quick Steps Enable your mobile hotspot. Click the Wi-Fi icon on your computer. Select your hotspot's name.
