In Windows, there are 2 ways you can adjust your volume balance settings: You can change how loud the left and right speakers or headphones are, or you can balance your app volume if one app is way too loud. In this step-by-step article, we'll cover how to do both methods. Keep reading to learn more.

How to Adjust Volume Balance Settings in Windows

To balance your speakers, go to Settings > System > Sound > More sound settings, select your headphones or speakers, then click Properties > Levels > Balance. To balance your app volume, click on the volume icon in your taskbar, then click the button next to the volume slider and scroll down to "Volume mixer." Steps Method 1 Method 1 of 2:
