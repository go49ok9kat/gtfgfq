If you need to delete a table from a Microsoft Word document, it's easy. The steps are different depending on whether you're using the desktop version of Word for Windows and Mac or Word on the web. Read on to learn how to remove a table from a Word document and what to do if the table can't be deleted. Quick Steps Click anywhere in the table.

Click the 4-directional arrow at the top-left corner.

Press Backspace or Delete to delete the table. Steps Section 1 of 4:

Click your cursor inside the table. When you click the table, a 4-directional arrow will appear at its top-left corner.

If you don't see the 4-directional arrow, you may not be in Print Layout view. Use this method if you're editing your Word document in your web browser instead of the desktop Word app. 2

Click any cell in the table. This selects the table and displays the Table Tools tab at the top. 3

Click the Layout tab. It's at the top of the window just below "Table Tools." 4

Click Delete Table on the toolbar. It's the icon of a table with a red X at its bottom corner. This deletes the table from your Word document.[3]XResearch source4 Advertisement Section 3 of 4: Delete a Table and Keep the Text Download Article 1

Click inside the table. If you want to keep the text from a table but not the table structure and borders, you can easily convert the table to text. Clicking a cell in the table displays the "Table Design" and "Layout" tabs at the top of Word. 2

Click the Layout tab. It's under "Table Tools" in the upper-right area of Word. 3

Click Convert to Text. It's on the right side of the toolbar at the top of Word. A list of conversion options will appear. 4

Select an option and click OK. If you're dealing with plain text, select the Tabs option to replace the table borders with tabs, making your text easier to read.

If you're working with data that you want to save as a CSV (Comma Separated Values) file, choose Commas instead. Advertisement Section 4 of 4: Can't Delete the Table? Download Article 1

Enable the Developer toolbar. If you can't delete a table, the creator may have protected it with Content Control. You can unlock this protection in the Developer toolbar. This feature is only available in the desktop version of Word for Windows and Mac, not on the web. To enable this toolbar: Click the File menu and select Options. Click Customize Ribbon.

In the "Customize the Ribbon" section, check the box next to "Developer" and click OK.[4]XTrustworthy SourceMicrosoft SupportTechnical support and product information from Microsoft.Go to source 2

Click the Developer tab. Now that you've enabled this feature, you'll see it at the top of Word. 3

Click anywhere in the table. This selects the table. 4

Click Properties in the toolbar. If the option is grayed out, click Design Mode first. You should now see a pop-up window called "Content Control Properties." 5

Uncheck "Content control cannot be deleted" and click OK. This removes protection from the table. You can now delete the table normally. Advertisement Expert Q&A  Search Add New Question Ask a Question 200 characters left
