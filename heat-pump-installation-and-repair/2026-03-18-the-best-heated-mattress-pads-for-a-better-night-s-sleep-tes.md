What to Consider When Choosing the Best Heated Mattress Pad

There are many qualities to consider when shopping for a heated mattress pad for a bed. Among the most important are the material, size, heating mechanism, temperature control, and safety features. Material/Fabric

Heated mattress pads typically come in three types of material: cotton, polyester, or a blend of the two. Cotton is softer and absorbent, so sleepers won’t wake up sweaty after sleeping in a heated bed. Polyester is stretchier, and while less absorbent, it is still breathable. Microfiber, a type of polyester, is another option for those who want a stretchy fabric with a plush feel. Blends can be the best of two or more materials.

When choosing a heating pad material, remember that a high-quality product will have a higher thread count, making it softer and less likely to pill. Size and Fit

Electric mattress pads come in the standard bed sizes of twin, full, queen, and king. Some come with an elastic skirt that will keep the pad in place throughout the night, and others are made like a fitted sheet and wrap firmly around the bed. The latter tends to be a better product, as it’s less likely to move or get bunched up in the night.

Before choosing a mattress pad, measure the depth of the mattress as well as its size. Mattresses range in thickness and can be anywhere from 5 inches deep to more than 16 inches deep. The mattress pad needs to fit the depth of the mattress. Heating Mechanism

Electric mattress pads get their heat from small wires spaced throughout the pad. Generally, the thinner the wires, the lower the voltage and the heat and the safer the pad. Some newer models of mattress pads have tubes that carry heated water instead of wires that carry voltage. These pads can be a safer alternative.

Wire spacing is another consideration, as the space between each wire affects the heat distribution. Pads with wires spaced several inches apart will distribute heat over a wider area of the pad, while pads with wires centered in one area will not. Temperature Control

Most heated mattress pads let the user control the temperature with a knob or a digital device. Temperature control on a high-quality pad will have multiple heating levels, including the ability to heat both sides of the pad individually. Some pads even have remote controllers.

Before purchasing, consider who will be using the mattress pad. Someone with poor eyesight might need a digital device with large, visible numbers, while someone with poor motor skills might prefer a push-button instead of a dial. No matter the kind of controller, it’s helpful when the bed’s temperature is clearly displayed and easy to read. Additional Features

Newer models come with a range of features and amenities to make a heated sleeping experience more enjoyable. Many high-quality electric mattress pads will have a timer that turns the pad off after a certain period to prevent accidents or overheating. Some pads allow for personalized heat settings, dual heating options, and multiple heat levels.

Another feature to consider is water resistance. A water-resistant pad will prevent sleepers from waking up in a pool of sweat, and it can protect the mattress and heating pad from spills. Safety

Look for products with a UL certification, which indicates the independent consumer safety organization Underwriters Laboratories has tested it. Most pads come with an automatic shut-off timer that shuts off the pad after a set time. Some newer models have a feature that shuts off the heating element in the mattress pad when its built-in sensor detects too much heat.

Some mattress pads also come in a lower voltage, which can be a better option for children, pregnant women, and those dealing with health or circulatory system issues. Ease of Cleaning

Like any type of mattress topper, a heated mattress pad that’s easy to clean, and one that isn’t easily damaged by cleaning, makes using it and maintaining it less time-consuming.

Before buying a mattress pad, check its cleaning instructions. Some are machine washable, but many recommend using the machine’s gentle or delicate cycle with the pad and washing it with cold water. Machine washable pads can typically be put in a clothes dryer or air-dried.

Other mattress pads can only be spot cleaned with mild soap, which requires more work.

Heated mattress pads are electric mattress pads that use wires or water tubes to heat a bed to a warmer temperature.

Q. Can you feel the wires in a heated mattress pad?

In a high-quality mattress pad, users do not feel the wires. They are sealed in a soft, quilted cover.

Q. How long does it take for a heated mattress pad to heat up?

It depends on the model, but most take 30 minutes to reach their maximum temperature.

Yes. However, the more layers between you and the heated mattress pad, the harder it will be to feel its warmth. Q. Can I wash my heated mattress pad?
