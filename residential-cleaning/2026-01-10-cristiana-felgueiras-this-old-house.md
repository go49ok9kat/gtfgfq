Woodworker Cristiana Felgueiras of GET HANDS DIRTY got started with a simple search: How to make a wood joint. As Cris learned, she became obsessed with the craft. Her home furniture designs have an inviting, modern aesthetic, built for maximum efficiency in small spaces. Cris currently creates from her studio in Porto, Portugal.
